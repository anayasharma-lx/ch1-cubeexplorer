1729 — the Hardy–Ramanujan number: Hardy arrived in a taxi numbered 1729 and called it "dull." Ramanujan instantly replied: "It is the smallest number expressible as the sum of two cubes in two different ways." Toggle between the two ways below — notice that both pairs of cubes have completely different sizes, yet both sum to exactly 1729.

Why remarkable
Most numbers can't be written as a sum of two cubes even once. 1729 does it twice — and it's the smallest number with this property.
