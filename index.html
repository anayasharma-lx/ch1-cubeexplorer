Hidden pattern

Hardy–Ramanujan
1729 is the smallest number expressible as the sum of two cubes in two different ways.
The taxicab number
1729
Hardy called it dull. Ramanujan disagreed.
Way 1
1³ + 12³
1 + 1,728 = 1729
Way 2
9³ + 10³
729 + 1,000 = 1729
Why remarkable
Most numbers can't be written as a sum of two cubes even once.
1729 does it twice — and it's the smallest such number.
